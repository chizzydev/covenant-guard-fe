Why this is different from a preset

A preset assumes an old state. The Goal Planner reads the actual Fe configuration and recalculates how to reach your requested outcome. If the same goal is already satisfied, it proposes no transaction. If a market is over its ceiling, Fe gives the rejection reason. If a new transaction changes the contract version, the old plan must be regenerated.

What this decision protects

Exposure containment

Fe verifies the affected market's exposure against the operator-entered incident limit during preview and again during the atomic commit. A guardian installs a persistent Fe obligation; operators cannot silently weaken or remove it.

Fe policy enforcement

The compiled Fe contract checks operation preconditions, version and signer, and its fixed final-state invariants. Temporary intermediate breaches are not final approvals.

Exact-outcome commitment

Fe commitGoalActions() asserts the chosen terminal state in the atomic transaction. The server re-assesses the alert before sending any transaction.
